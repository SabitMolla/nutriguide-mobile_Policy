Who We Are

Nutriguide ("we", "our", or "us") is a mobile application developed and operated by an independent developer. The app is available on Android (Google Play) under the package name com.sabit.nutriguidemobile.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal health data responsibly and transparently. By using Nutriguide, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

02

Information We Collect

To generate your personalized nutrition and workout plans, we collect the following categories of information:

👤

Personal Profile

Age, gender, height, and current body weight. This is required to calculate your daily caloric needs and fitness goals.

🎯

Fitness Goals

Your body goal (e.g., muscle gain, weight loss), target weight, activity level, workout days, and session duration.

🏥

Medical Conditions

Any medical conditions or physical limitations you voluntarily provide to help tailor safer recommendations.

📷

Food Photos

Images you submit for AI-based food and calorie analysis. Photos are sent to a third-party AI API and are not stored by us.

🍽️

Food Log

Daily meal entries including food descriptions, calorie counts, and macronutrient data you log through the app.

📱

Device Info

Basic device and app interaction data collected automatically for app functionality and crash reporting (OS version, device model).

We collect only the minimum data necessary for the app to function. We never collect your real name, phone number, or financial information.

04

AI Processing & Third-Party APIs

Nutriguide uses AI language and vision models to generate personalized plans and analyze food. The following third-party services process your data as part of this functionality:

🤖

Google AI Studio — Gemma 4 Model

Your health profile data and food images are sent to Google AI Studio to generate nutrition plans, workout plans, goal recommendations, and food analysis results. When you submit a food photo, that image is transmitted to Google's servers for processing. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google API Services User Data Policy. Nutriguide does not store your food images on its own servers.

⚡

Groq API — Text Processing (Fallback)

In some cases, text-based requests (such as initial profile setup or goal recommendations) may be processed via the Groq API using large language models. The data sent is limited to your anonymized health profile attributes (no personally identifying information).

We strongly encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third-party providers. Nutriguide is not responsible for the data practices of external API providers once data is transmitted to their services.

05

Advertising (Unity Ads)

Nutriguide displays rewarded advertisements through Unity Ads (provided by Unity Technologies). These ads allow you to unlock additional daily food scans and features for free.

Unity Ads may collect the following information to serve ads:

Advertising ID (GAID) Device model & OS version IP address (approximate location) App interaction data Ad view and click events

Unity Ads operates independently from Nutriguide's data collection. You can opt out of personalized advertising by resetting your Google Advertising ID in your Android device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

For more information, please review the Unity Technologies Privacy Policy.

06

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in two places:

📱

On Your Device (Local Storage)

Your full health profile, nutrition plan, workout plan, and food log are stored locally on your device using secure device storage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use an AI feature.

☁️

Cloud (Future / Optional)

If cloud sync features are enabled, data may be stored securely using Firebase / Firestore with industry-standard AES-256 encryption at rest. You will be explicitly informed before any cloud sync is enabled.

We implement the following security measures:

  • All data transmitted to AI APIs is sent over HTTPS with TLS encryption.
  • We do not maintain any backend server that stores your personal data.
  • Food images sent for analysis are not retained or logged by us after the API call completes.
  • No passwords or financial information are ever collected.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing or commercial purposes. Data sharing is strictly limited to what is necessary to operate the app:

Third Party What is Shared Why
Google AI Studio Health profile attributes, food images AI plan generation & food analysis
Groq API Anonymized health profile attributes (text only) AI text generation (fallback)
Unity Technologies Device advertising ID, app interaction data Displaying rewarded video ads
Legal Authorities Data required by valid legal order Compliance with applicable law

We never share your data with data brokers, analytics platforms, or marketing agencies.

08

Data Retention

Because your data is stored locally on your device, you are in full control of its retention:

  • Your profile and food log data remain on your device until you manually delete your account or uninstall the app.
  • Food images submitted for analysis are processed in real time and are not retained by us after the response is received.
  • If cloud sync is active, you can request deletion of your cloud data at any time by contacting us.
  • AI-generated plans (nutrition plan, workout plan) are stored locally and are deleted when you reset your profile or uninstall the app.

Your Rights

Depending on your location (including users in the EU/EEA under GDPR, California users under CCPA, and others), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • 🔍

    Right to Access
    Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

  • ✏️

    Right to Rectification
    Correct any inaccurate or incomplete data in your profile directly within the app.

  • 🗑️

  • ↩️

    Right to Withdraw Consent
    Stop using any feature at any time. Your consent can be withdrawn by discontinuing use of that feature.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address listed in Section 12.
